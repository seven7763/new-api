核心隐私承诺

本服务不主动存储您的对话正文。AI 请求与响应在转发完成后即被丢弃，仅保留必要的元数据（请求时间、Token 用量等）用于计费与排障。我们遵循最小必要原则收集与处理您的信息。

第三方共享与上游数据流向

我们不出售您的个人信息。仅在以下情形向第三方传输必要信息：

  • AI 模型服务商：为完成您的请求，我们会将请求内容（对话 / Prompt）转发给 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google、Mistral 等上游服务商。这些服务商按其自身隐私政策处理数据，本服务无法控制其留存与使用方式。
  • 支付处理方：充值时，支付信息由独立的支付服务商处理（如 Stripe、加密货币网关等），我们通常仅接收支付结果与订单号。
  • 云基础设施：服务部署于境外云服务商，数据传输与存储受其安全机制保护。
  • 法律要求：在收到合法、有效的司法或行政机关请求时，我们可能依法披露必要信息。
重要提醒：您输入的内容会被上游模型服务商接收并按其政策处理（部分服务商可能用于安全审核或模型改进）。请避免向 AI 输入敏感、隐私或机密信息。

How We Use Information

04

Third-Party Sharing & Upstream Data Flows

We do not sell your personal information. We transfer only the necessary information to third parties in the following cases:

  • Payment processors: during top-up, payment information is handled by independent payment providers (such as Stripe or crypto gateways); we typically receive only the payment result and order number.
  • Cloud infrastructure: the Service is deployed on overseas cloud providers, and data transmission and storage are protected by their security mechanisms.
  • Legal requirements: upon a lawful, valid request from a judicial or administrative authority, we may disclose necessary information as required by law.
Important: your input is received and processed by upstream model providers under their policies (some providers may use it for safety review or model improvement). Please avoid submitting sensitive, private or confidential information to the AI.

  • Access control: we follow the principle of least privilege and retain necessary operation and audit records for key actions
